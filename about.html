I started with communication design, but over time I found myself more interested in how things work than how they look. That shift pulled me toward product design.

I enjoy starting ideas and figuring things out from scratch, but I also spend a lot of time refining the small details that shape how something actually feels to use. Motion plays a big role in how I think through interactions.

One thing that bothers me is design without context. I think good design comes from understanding the problem first, visuals come after. A lot of work looks good but doesn't really hold up when you ask why it exists.

Outside of work, I like cats, photography, playing guitar, collecting watches, IoT & long drives.
